similar cases as regards categories of precedent cases in trustees of the port of karachi versus muhammad saleem scmr it was observed that precedent cases fall in two distinct categories in the first category fall the decisions which decide a question of law or are based upon or enunciate a principle of law within the meaning of articles and of the constitution and are therefore binding if the decision be by the supreme court on all courts in pakistan and if the decision be by a high court on all courts subordinate to it in the second category fall the cases which are not so binding but are merely illustrations of the application of the principles of law enunciated in the first category of precedent cases so in order that a decision on a question of law is binding within the meaning of articles and of the constitution it is not enough that a legal proposition follows logically from it rather that question must have been actually decided it is pertinent to mention here that in recent
