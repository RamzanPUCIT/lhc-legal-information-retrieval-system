of pakistan held as under when perusing an order under section of the act the ccp is not required to give a detailed reasoned explanation to the undertaking as to the enquiry but should have deliberated on the issue so as to come to the conclusion that an enquiry is necessary the gist of which reasoning should be communicated to the undertaking in writing the internal working documents of the ccp need not be communicated in their entirety to the undertaking whilst at the same time its intent along with gist of the reasoning to conduct an enquiry must be communicated the petitioners inception of the inquiry the petitioners persistence on this ground is thus untenable the challenge to the impugned notices of on the basis that they have no nexus with the subject matter is equally interfere in the investigative stage additionally the impugned notices are not a final order they do not determine any of the petitioners rights nor do they impose any penalty they are simply notices for information in essence the enquiry conducted under section functions as a preliminary stage aimed at collecting information and evidence if this process indicates a possible breach of chapter ii of the act the ccp may then take further action however conducting such an enquiry is not a mandatory prerequisite for wp no of proceeding against an undertaking if the ccp is satisfied based on the information already available that a contravention of chapter ii has occurred it may proceed directly without conducting an enquiry in the dalda foods case supra the supreme court explicated that the ccp being a regulator is tasked with and as such empowered to collect market information to understand market structure therefore providing such information to ccp by an undertaking is not considered an adverse action against the undertaking the petitioner if believes it is not involved in any anticompetitive practice its remedy is not to rush to this court but to provide the requisite information this fact as discussed above the impugned notices only sought certain information from the petitioner relating to an already pending inquiry by no stretch of imagination the impugned notices can be and is accordingly dismissed before parting with this order it is observed that although the act does not prescribe any statutory timeframe for conclusion of an inquiry under section principle of
