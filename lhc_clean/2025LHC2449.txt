decree dated passed by learned civil judge st class faisalabad concisely the facts giving rise to the present civil revision are that petitionerplaintiff filed a suit for possession through specific performance against respondent nodefendant on the basis of agreement to sell dated exp hereinafter called agreement through the initial plaint petitionerplaintiff sought specific performance of the agreement regarding prospective share out of khewat no situated at chak no b tehsil and district faisalabad was mentioned but thereafter he sought amendment in the cr no plaint beyond the property mentioned in the agreement by including other khewats no and their land which was allowed by learned trial court respondent nodefendant after appearing before learned trial court and moving a miscellaneous application disappeared later on and thus ex parte evidence was recorded resultantly suit of the petitionerplaintiff was ex parte decreed vide judgment and decree dated feeling aggrieved respondent nodefendant preferred an appeal which was partially accepted by learned appellate court vide judgment and decree dated therefore this civil revision learned counsel for the petitioner contends that learned appellate court granted the decree on the point which was never raised in the appeal before it and as such the same was in violation of order xli rule cpc adds that if learned appellate court wanted to decide the appeal beyond the grounds raised therein it should have issued notice in this respect to the petitioner at the fag end of his arguments learned counsel prays that in these circumstances the case be remanded to learned appellate court for decision afresh after issuing him notice under order xli rule cpc conversely respondent no who has opted to argue the case herself has supported the judgment and decree passed by learned appellate court and prays for dismissal of the instant civil revision heard record perused according to order vi rule cpc pleadings can be amended at any stage of the proceedings for ready reference the said provision is reproduced below amendment of pleading the court may at any stage of the proceedings allow either party to alter or amend his pleadings in such manner and on such terms as may be just and all such amendments shall be made as may be necessary for the purpose of determining the real questions in controversy between the parties cr no definition of pleading is provided in order vi rule cpc for ready reference the said provision is reproduced below pleading pleading shall mean plaint or written statement the supreme court of pakistan in its judgment reported as muhammad munir and others v umar hayat and others scmr has emphatically held that a party could not go beyond the scope of its pleadings it is also a settled principle of law that a party cannot be permitted to lead evidence in respect of a plea not taken in the pleadings or to put it in another manner a party has first to plead and then to prove the said plea in this regard it would be expedient to refer to celebrated principlemaxim of law ie secundum allegata etprobata a party can only succeed according to what was alleged and proved reliance can be placed upon amir shah v ziarat gul scmr and ch rab nawaz v mst nasreen and others ylr in addition to above it shall be important to mention here that pleading per se is not evidence rather pleading is the basic engine of litigation and whole controversy revolves around it light can be taken from the dicta laid down in muhammad nawaz alias nawaza and others v member judicial board of revenue and others scmr and messrs essa engineering company pvt ltd and another v pakistan telecommuncation company limited and another scmr and muhammad iqbal v mehboob alam scmr placing the aforenoted caselaw in juxtaposition with the facts of present case it is very much clear and conspicuous that under order vi rule cpc court can only allow amendment in the pleadings and not the basic documents which have been executed amongst the parties in the present case learned trial court allowed the petitionerplaintiff an amendment which is beyond the basic document ie agreement exp the terms of an agreement contract or cr no document could only be amended or negotiated by the parties themselves with their mutual consent and as such it was not the mandate of learned trial court to allow any amendment in the terms and conditions of the agreement against the will of concerned party in the garb of amendment in the pleadings in this scenario it can safely be said that learned trial court passed the judgment and decree beyond the jurisdiction vested in it epitome of the above discussion is that this court does not find any illegality legal infirmity jurisdictional defect misreading non reading of record in the impugned judgment and decree to which no exception can be taken by this court in revisional jurisdiction with this observation this civil revision being devoid of merits stands dismissed malik waqar haider awan judge signed approved for reporting judge abis ali
