essential to state that the courts as a repository of justice and in exercise of judicial power of the state are obligated to dispense and administer justice and in the performance of this sacrosanct doctrine of unjust enrichment aims at correction of the injustice that occurred when the claimant suffered a subtraction of wealth and some other individual generally a defendant in a case received a corresponding benefit the doctrine of unjust enrichment has been welldeveloped in all systems of civil jurisprudence including france canada english and american in pakistan and india this doctrine is embodied in the contract act the basis of the doctrine is that if a person has received any property or benefit from another it is just that he should make restitution as otherwise he would be unjustly enriched at the expense of the other keeping these principles in mind if the facts of this case are examined it reveals that the petitioner did apply for permission to deposit the balance
