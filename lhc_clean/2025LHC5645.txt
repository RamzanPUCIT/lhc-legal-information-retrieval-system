revolves around the interpretation and application of writ petition no of page rule of order vi of the code of civil procedure cpc which reads as under amendment of pleadings the court may at any stage of the proceedings allow either party to alter or amend his pleadings in such manner and on such terms as may be just and all such amendments shall be made as may be necessary for the purpose of determining the real questions in controversy between the parties plain reading of above provision suggests that an application for amendment of pleadings under rule ibid can be moved at any stage of proceedings however authority of the court under the aforementioned rule remains discretionary this is manifest not only from the use of word may but also in such manner and on such terms as may be just in that provision nonetheless guideline has been provided by the legislature to the effect that only such amendments to the pleadings are to be allowed which are necessary for determining real questions in controversy between the parties the discretionary power under rule ibid is however further guided by principles enunciated by the superior courts from time to time the supreme court of pakistan in the case of abaid ullah malik v additional district judge mianwali and others pld sc elaborated some of the principles governing exercise of such discretionary power in the following terms there can be no cavil that the court has ample authority and discretion to allow amendments of the pleadings in appropriate cases which authority may be exercised at any stage of the proceedings in amendments shall be liberally writ petition no of page exercised however keeping in view the salutary rule that the proposed amendments isare expedient for the purposes of determining the real be kept in mind by the courts while exercising such authority in that the amendment soughtproposed must not be tainted with dishonesty of purposes it is not meant to withdraw and resile from an
