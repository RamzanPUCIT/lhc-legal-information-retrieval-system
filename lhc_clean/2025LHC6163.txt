writ petition no of naseeb masih president of pakistan etc petitioner in person mr waqas umar aag mr rafaqat ali dogar deputy attorney general and syed muhammad farhad tirmizi deputy attorney general through the instant petition the petitioner seeks direction to the respondents to consider appointing personnel from minority communities as judges of the high courts to fill existing vacancies in view of the provisions of the constitution of the islamic republic of pakistan at the very outset the petitioner has pressed the instant petition only to the extent that quota for minorities be fixed in the superior judiciary in view of the judgment of the apex court reported as pld sc and rule of the judicial commission of pakistan heard learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that quota for minorities in the superior judiciary be fixed it is relevant to mention here that the judicial service has been assigned a specific place within the constitutional framework it would neither be appropriate to bring it under the purview of the establishment nor to fix a quota in it as per the constitution of the islamic republic of pakistan a procedure for the appointment of judges of the superior judiciary has been prescribed which is equal for all chapter of part vii of the constitution deals with the appointment of judges to the high courts article a provides that there shall be a judicial commission of pakistan for the writ petition no of appointment of judges of the supreme court high courts and the federal shariat court subclause of article a of the constitution mandates that the commission may make rules regulating its procedure including the procedures and criteria for the assessment evaluation and fitness for appointment of judges the judicial commission of pakistan framed rules in exercise of the powers conferred by clause of article a of the constitution of the islamic republic of pakistan the power to appoint a judge of the high court vests with the judicial commission of pakistan wherein the merit of all candidates for appointment will be equal and rule of the judicial commission of pakistan provides that the members of the commission shall subject to the prescribed criteria ensure proper diversity in terms of gender region and religion in initiating and finalizing the nominations for the appointment of judges in the high courts the judicial commission of pakistan considers merit and also looks into the diversity of nominations in fact the petitioner mainly focused on the quota for minorities the judicial service is not a traditional form of employment and is separate from other services members of other services cannot be equated with members of the judiciary either constitutionally or functionally therefore the quota cannot be considered to prioritize the judiciary or the high court it would hence not be appropriate to compromise on merit given the above the instant petition being devoid of any merits stands dismissed aalia neelum chief justice approved for reporting chief justice this order has been dictated pronounced prepared and signed on ikram
