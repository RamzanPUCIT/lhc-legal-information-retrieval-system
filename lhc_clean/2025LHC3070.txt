government of punjab and others v ms shamim usman scmr the officer filed a constitutional petition before the high court seeking a direction to the department to finalize her promotion case and to the relevant excerpt is reproduced below the high court therefore has no jurisdiction to entertain any proceedings in respect of terms and conditions of service of a civil servant which can be civil servant to an appointment or promotion in the province of punjab v hafiz muhammad kaleemud din scmr a matter arising under the punjab civil servants act a provincial law the civil servant filed constitutional court should always be mindful of the jurisdictional exclusion contained under article of the constitution any transgression of this constitutional limitation will render its order void and illegal therefore unless the jurisdiction of the service writ petition no tribunal is ousted under section b of the sta as described above the assumption of jurisdiction by the high court in respect of matters of terms and conditions of a civil servant is unconstitutional and impermissible in the present case the petitioner submitted a representation seeking consideration for promotion to
