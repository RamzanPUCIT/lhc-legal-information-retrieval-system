perusal of above reproduced scheme of law reveals that the act of deals concerned with the registration of poultry premises regulation of poultry production and enhancement of biosecurity measures section j defines poultry premises to include farms hatcheries processing plants and diagnostic facilities section h clarifies that a poultry farm refers to the premises for growing birds and expressly excludes temporary sale points section mandates registration as a condition precedent for operations the act of by contrast is designed as a food safety statute its preamble makes clear its object to protect public health and regulate food safety and standards section provides that no person shall operate a food business without prescribed registration or license section vests in the authority a power to exempt a class of food operators from this requirement it is evident that the two statutes operate in distinct but complementary spheres the act of regulates the production environment and standards applicable to poultry farming the act of intervenes at the point where food is introduced into the consumer supply chain the sale of eggs for human consumption falls squarely within the ambit of the latter the petitioner company contention that compliance with the act of insulates it from regulation under the act of cannot be accepted such a construction would defeat the purpose of the act of which is premised on the regulatory imperative of ensuring food safety while there may be regulatory overlap it is trite that overlap is not repugnancy the two statutes serve distinct legislative objectives and may validly coexist furthermore there is no ambiguity in the proposition that a company incorporated under the companies act may follow different sets of statutes like tax law labour law wpno of and environmental law being regulated by distinct regulatory authorities simultaneously the issuance of the impugned notice is not without jurisdiction the petitioner upon engaging in the sale of eggs is operating a food business within the meaning of section of the act of the food authority was therefore acting within its statutory mandate in requiring the petitioner to obtain a license furthermore no document showing exemption under section of the act of is available on the record the petitioner companys claim of not using its premises as a sale point is an attempt to evade the jurisdiction of the respondents however when questioned about other registered sale points he denied their existence the court finds it implausible that the petitioner would handle such a large quantity of eggs without selling them for human consumption casting doubt on his claims in view of above discussion this court addresses the legal question framed supra by holding that the petitioner company being licensed under the punjab poultry production act for poultry farming is still subject to licensing requirement under section of punjab food authority act when it engages in the sale of eggs for human consumption accordingly instant writ petition is dismissed syed ahsan raza kazmi judge approved for reporting judge amjad
