the wake of availability of alternate remedy constitutional petition cannot be entertained we have carefully gone through the record and considered the submission of learned counsel for the parties before going into merits of the case we feel it apt to firstly decide regarding maintainability of this petition and jurisdiction of this court to adjudicate upon the matter the supreme court of pakistan in judgment reported as muslim commercial bank limited v muhammad anwar mandokhel scmr has held that every court prior to taking cognizance of and adjudicating upon an issue should first resort wp no to the question of assumption of jurisdiction of the court if it comes to the conclusion that jurisdiction can be assumed only then it can adjudicate upon the issue it is important to note that vide judgment dated rendered by this division bench in writ petition no titled rukhsana yasmeen etc v province of punjab through secretary public prosecution department etc it has already been settled that except the post of prosecutor general rest of the employees of prosecution service are governed under the punjab civil servants act hereinafter called act and rules made thereunder and as such they fall in the definition of civil servants as regards the argument advanced by learned counsel for the petitioners that petitioners leaving aside the point whether they are civil servants or public servants can always invoke the constitutional jurisdiction of this court for challenging the legality of law notification amendment or rules introduced by government of the punjab we are afraid that this is not the correct legal position we can take light from the dictum laid down in judgment rendered by a larger bench of the supreme court of pakistan reported as ia sharwani and others v government of pakistan through secretary finance division islamabad and others scmr wherein while interpreting section of the service tribunals act it has categorically been held that appropriate remedy for a civil servant is by way of an appeal before the service tribunal even where the case involves vires of a particular service rule or a notification it was further held that if a statutory rule or notification adversely affects terms and conditions of a civil servant the same will be treated as a final order for the purposes of jurisdiction of a service tribunal it was held downrightly that a civil servant cannot bypass the jurisdiction of the service tribunal by adding a ground of violation of fundamental rights wp no we are fortified with the view taken in the recent venerated judgment of the supreme court of pakistan reported as muhammad hassanullah omgb acting additional secretary health department balochistan v chief secretary government of balochistan quetta and another scmr wherein the case of ia sharwani and others supra has been discussed in detail and it has been unambiguously held that the exclusive jurisdiction of the service tribunal and the bar contained under article of the constitution are of such a nature that they are attracted even if the grievance arises from an order which may involve questions of mala fide corum non judice or having been passed without jurisdiction the dicta laid down in ia sharwani and others case has explicitly been endorsed by the supreme court of pakistan in case of muhammad hassanullah supra with a further clarification that if a statutory rule or notification adversely affects terms and conditions of a civil servant the same will be treated as a final order and for adjudicating upon the same exclusive jurisdiction lies with an administrative tribunal established under the command of article of the constitution for the sake of arguments if we borrow the idea of learned counsel for the petitioners that civil servants can approach this court for challenging the vires of any law or notification then there would be anomalous situation as civil servants will approach this court for their personalservice grievances in the garb of violation of fundamental rights which cannot be encouraged apart from this the language used by the legislature in article of the constitution makes article of the constitution subservient to it and gives article an overriding effect over the other articles of the constitution qua the enforcement of terms and conditions of service of a civil servant as the jurisdiction exercised by this court under article of the constitution is subject to the constitution therefore article of the constitution ousts the jurisdiction of this court in the cases pertaining to enforcement of terms and conditions of services of a civil servant reliance can be wp no placed on the judgments of ali azhar khan baloch and others v province of sindh and others scmr and national assembly secretariat v manzoor ahmed and another scmr we are clear in our mind that while exercising constitutional jurisdiction under article of the constitution this court has to see not only the bar contained in article of the constitution regarding maintainability of petition but also the language contained in article of the constitution even otherwise if vires of a notification or amendment in law dealing directly or indirectly with terms and conditions of service of a civil servant are permitted to be challenged through a constitutional petition it will amount to violating article of the constitution and section of punjab civil servants act hereinafter called act in addition to above if this dispensation is sanctioned there would be no uniformity with regard to the constitutional jurisdiction of this court and powers of the punjab service tribunal and in this way it can safely be said that it would not be a harmonious interpretation of law it is a settled principle that when law requires an act to be done in a particular manner then it has to be done in that manner and not otherwise in this respect reliance is placed upon muhammad akram v mst zainab bibi scmr muhammad anwar and others v mst ilyas begum and others pld supreme court and sheikh ghulam hussain v chief settlement commissioner and others ylr learned counsel for the petitioners has placed much stress on judgment reported as province of sindh through chief secretary sindh sindh secretariat and another v prosecutorgeneral sindh criminal prosecution department and others scmr it is noteworthy that this judgment was comprehensively discussed in muzaffar ali anjum and others v government of punjab through wp no secretary ministry of law and parliamentary affairs lahore and others plc cs wherein comparison of act rules and the sindh criminal prosecution service constitution functions and powers act hereinafter called act has been given it is important to note that act is not followed by any rules and the aforementioned acts and rules are entirely different from one another although section of the act provides that members of service except the prosecutor general including the staff shall be appointed and covered under the sindh civil servants act and rules made thereunder but the act is not followed by any rules whereas the act is followed by the rules which manifestly clarifies the provisions of the act that employees of the punjab prosecution department are civil servants in support of his contentions learned counsel for the petitioners has also stressed on section of the act which provides that a prosecutor appointed under the act shall be deemed to be a public servant within the meaning of section of the pakistan penal code it would be important to mention here that public servant is a broader term which includes not only civil servants but also other government entities like armed forces judiciary and statutory corporations thus it could be said that every civil servant is a public servant because he serves the public and is paid salary from the public exchequer but every public servant is not a civil servant in order to draw distinction between civil servant and public servant it would be expedient to see how their recruitmentappointment is made in the case in hand employees of the public prosecution department punjab are appointed under the act and regulated by the rules rule of the rules provides that instructions issued and directions given under the act would be applicable for the terms and conditions of service therefore indubitably they are civil servants which has already been settled by this division bench in the case of rukhsana yasmeen etc supra wp no in the light of foregoing facts and law it is concluded that being civil servants appropriate remedy for the petitioners to voice their grievance relating to terms and conditions of service and to challenge the vitality of any law including notification and amendment introduced by government of the punjab is to approach the punjab service tribunal instead of invoking constitutional jurisdiction of this court consequently this petition is dismissed being
