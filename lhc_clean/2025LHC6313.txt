unexceptionable heard record minutely examined first of all we extend our considerations towards dissimilarity between upgradation of post and promotion in pursuit to a just and equitable resolution of the present controversy wisdom is sought from the dictum laid down in secretary to the government of pakistan ica no establishment division islamabad and another v muhammad ahmed khan and others scmr wherein distinction has been drawn between upgradation and promotion relevant extract from paragraph no is reproduced below we are mindful that the upgradation of a post is not a vested right rather it stems from a policy decision intended to benefit a particular set of employees under the scheme embedded in the policy upgradation cannot be mixed up with promotion in the case of upgradation the employee continues to hold the same post without any change in his duties but he is accorded a higher pay scale in order to mitigate the distress associated with stagnation due to a lack of progression or promotional avenues emphasis supplied in the above backdrop we can safely say that the terms upgradation and promotion are entirely different from each other it is imperative to note that dispute arose when notification dated qua upgradationredesignation of posts of appellants and others was withdrawn as a result of clarification dated issued by government of the punjab finance department meaning thereby cause of conflict is the clarification relevant portion of which is reproduced hereunder for ready reference in case more than one tiers were available prior to issuance of notifications dated these tiers would be restructured according to the said notifications through amendment in the service rules and restructuredupgraded posts would be filled by promotion or fresh recruitment as per revised prescribed qualifications mentioned in the above said notifications we are clear in our mind that service rules can always be amalgamated and amended by the competent authority but same are applicable to promotion only the purpose of service rules with regard to promotion is to provide fair opportunities to progress in life and profession based upon seniority and performance on the other hand in the case of upgradation the incumbent continues to hold the same post without any alteration in duties or responsibilities however a higher pay ica no scale is conferred upon him with a view to alleviating stagnation and addressing the hardship arising from the absence of promotional or career advancement opportunities moreover the purpose of upgradation is to eliminate the sufferings of employees from stagnation and to avoid them from being stuck up in some isolated post without any pathway or probability of promotion despite satisfactory length of service in a particular post in addition to above upgradation is the policy to improve the life of incumbents of post it is a key to instigate a person to work hard article of the constitution ordains that every citizen shall be dealt with equally and there shall be no discrimination article of the constitution talks about right to life which includes hope to progress equal opportunities to progress it is devastating even to think that a person starts his professional career in a same grade and finishes his or her professional life in that very grade the pivotal question before us is whether even if service rules were in existence upgradation would nonetheless remain a necessity of the day to enable employees to make both ends meet in the light of prevailing price hike and inflationary trends even assuming that service rules had been framed the object and purpose of upgradation would still be to compensate incumbents for the impact of inflation and the rising cost of living to attract and retain competent personnel and to motivate the workforce upgradation therefore has no nexus with the service rules which are designed to ensure fair opportunities for career progression the criterion for promotion is ordinarily based on seniority cumfitness whereas the purpose of upgradation is the overall welfare of employees upgradation is not personspecific it is postspecific and general in nature conversely the service rules aim to provide a structured mechanism for competition and advancement within the service in a civilized and competitive system it is generally considered imperative to provide clear pathways for career progression in the ica no absence of promotional rules or opportunities employees may perceive the system as unfair which in turn leads to dissatisfaction and diminished motivation the absence of upgradation of posts when coupled with the lack of service rules governing promotion inevitably results in employee stagnation a sense of being undervalued decreased productivity and a potential decline in innovation the legitimate expectation of progression constitutes the cornerstone of every service profession or post when we
