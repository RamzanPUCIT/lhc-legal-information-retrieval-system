before the family court the law governing jurisdiction of family courts is the family courts act hereinafter referred to as the act of reference may first be made to the preamble of the act which reflects its objective to ensure expeditious settlement and disposal of disputes relating to marriage family affairs and wp no of matters connected therewith for convenience the preamble is reproduced as under preamble whereas it is expedient to make provision for the establishment of family courts for the expeditious settlement and disposal of disputes relating to marriage and family affairs and for matters connected therewith a plain reading of the preamble shows that family courts are established for effective and swift resolution of disputes concerning i marriage ii family affairs iii matters connected therewith section of the act of deals with jurisdiction of family courts relevant subsection whereof reads jurisdiction subject to the provisions of the muslim family laws ordinance and the conciliation courts ordinance the family courts shall have exclusive jurisdiction to entertain hear and adjudicate upon matters specified in part i of the schedule a bare perusal of subsection ibid reveals that jurisdiction of the family court extends to matters enumerated in parti of the schedule for convenience parti of the schedule is reproduced hereunder schedule see section part i dissolution of marriage including khula dower maintenance restitution of conjugal rights custody of children and the visitation rights of parents to meet them guardianship a matters pertaining to return of child under the hague convention on the civil aspects of international child abduction jactitation of marriage dowry wp no of personal property and belongings of a wife and a child living with his mother any other matter arising out of the nikahnama the interpretation of serial no which simply states maintenance is vital it is a wellestablished rule of construction that the legislature is never presumed to be short of words if the legislative intent had been to restrict the family courts jurisdiction solely to the maintenance of wife or child it would have explicitly written maintenance of wife and child at serial no as it has adopted such specific restrictive language at serial no of the schedule ibid as well as in section a of the act of the deliberate omission of any qualifying or restrictive language in serial no of the schedule is therefore highly relevant and manifest of the legislative intention that the entry should not be confined the purpose of the act of as is evident from its scheme and structure is to provide a specialized forum for the expeditious settlement of disputes relating to marriage family affairs and matters connected therewith a claim of maintenance by dependent parents against their sons being a family dispute rooted in familial obligations squarely falls within the domain of family affairs and is therefore well within the jurisdiction of the family court to read entry no as confined only to wife and child would be to read into the statute a restriction that the legislature consciously omitted as statutory provisions do not expressly define the nature of relationship between the person claiming maintenance and the party against whom maintenance is sought principles of islamic law which are the primary source governing family relations must be applied under islamic jurisprudence the obligation of sons to maintain their dependent parents squarely falls within the scope of maintenance support for this position can be found in para of the principles of muhammadan law by df mulla which categorically affirms the responsibility of children to maintain their indigent parents for ease of reference para is reproduced below maintenance of parents children in easy circumstances are bound to maintain their poor parents although the latter may be able to earn something for themselves wp no of a son though in straitened circumstances is bound to maintain his mother if the mother is poor though she may not be infirm a son who though poor is earning something is bound to support his poor father who earns nothing it is also a wellrecognized principle that family courts possess jurisdiction to decide all ancillary issues necessary for complete and effective adjudication of disputes within their domain the schedule is inclusive and must be interpreted purposively to advance the true spirit and objective of the statute consequently this court has no hesitation in holding that a mother or father if left dependent and without means can competently invoke jurisdiction of the family court to seek maintenance from their sons having held that a mother if left dependent and without sufficient means may competently invoke the jurisdiction of the family court to seek maintenance from her sons the court now turns to examine the legality of the impugned orders for proper appreciation section a of the act of is reproduced as under a suit for maintenance in a suit for maintenance the family court shall on the date of the first appearance of the defendant fix interim monthly maintenance for wife or a child and if the defendant fails to pay the maintenance by fourteen day of each month the defence of the defendant shall stand struck off and the family court shall decree the suit for maintenance on the basis of averments in the plaint and other supporting documents on record of the case the plain reading of section a ibid demonstrates that the legislature crafted a special and expedited mechanism to safeguard the rights of wives and children the provision is summary in nature timebound in its operation and is supported by the serious penal consequence of striking off the defence as this provision is expressly restricted by the legislature to the maintenance of a wife or a child it cannot be extended by necessary implication to encompass the suit for maintenance filed by a mother against her sons the specific inclusion of wife or a child indicates that the legislature intended to reserve this stringent summary power exclusively wp no of for those two categories since the provision visits a defendant with serious penal consequences it must be construed strictly and applied only in cases that squarely fall within its narrow statutory contours therefore section a is not attracted to the present dispute wherein a mother has sought maintenance from her sons however it is observed for clarity that the authority to pass a final decree for maintenance inherently includes the power to grant interim relief pendente lite as an ancillary or necessary measure to prevent destitution during litigation therefore the family court is fully empowered to fix and direct the payment of interim maintenance during the pendency of a suit filed by any parent and enforce such judicial orders exercising its powers under section a of the act of as well as inherent powers and every endeavor should be made to decide such cases within the period of six months prescribed in section a of the act ibid however the critical distinction must be maintained that the special and stringent penal consequences such as the striking off of the defense and the resultant decreeing of the suit for failure to pay the interim amount as provided exclusively under section a of the act of cannot be pressed into service by the family court in a suit for maintenance filed by parents against their sons in the instance case the judge family court directed the defendants through order dated to pay interim maintenance by the th day of each month however coercive measures were initiated on the rd day of the following month even before the expiry of the prescribed period this precipitate action reflects a clear misapprehension of the jurisdiction vested under section a ibid and amounts to denial of due process the additional district judge also failed to appreciate this legal infirmity and erroneously affirmed the order dated thereby perpetuating the defect it would not be out of place to observe that learned counsel for the petitioner during the course of arguments also raised an objection to the maintainability of the suit before the family court on the ground that it constitutes a second suit between the parties however such objection had wp no of not been pressed through any specific prayer in the present writ petition any adjudication on such factual controversy at this stage may effectively result in this court assuming the role of a court of first instance thereby prejudicing the right of appeal of the party who may feel aggrieved by such determination thus findings on the above factual objection are deliberately eschewed and the matter is left open to be examined and decided by the judge family court in accordance with law since the question as to whether a mother can maintain a suit for her maintenance against her sons before the family court involves interpretation of statutory provisions and such issue being purely of legal in nature is likely to recur in other cases concerning dependent parents seeking enforcement of their right to maintenance therefore to prevent uncertainty and conflicting views across different fora this court has considered it appropriate to conclusively determine the said legal question for the foregoing reasons instant petition is allowed consequently the impugned judgment and decree dated passed by the additional district judge as well as the order and decree dated passed by the judge family court along with all proceedings taken in pursuance thereof are hereby set aside the matter is remanded to the judge family court where the
