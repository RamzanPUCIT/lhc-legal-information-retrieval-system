the right of access to justice to all is a fundamental right guaranteed under articles and of the constitution of the islamic republic of pakistan ensuring every individual including those in custody can approach the courts for legal remedies a person detained in jail in connection with a criminal case cannot appear in a civil court to defend or prosecute a civil suit unless the civil court orders his production this restriction if not addressed by the court can lead to denial of fair trial and violation of natural justice to prevent such injustice courts have the power to summon the detained person thereby enabling their participation in civil proceedings ensuring access to justice for those in custody upholds the principles of equality due process and fair hearing denial of this right would amount to a serious infringement of constitutional guarantees this fundamental right of access to justice has been recognized and explained by the honble supreme court of pakistan in a case titled mehram ali and others v federation of pakistan and others pld sc taking stock of the above discussion it is clear that access to justice must be ensured even for individuals in custody failure of cr no of the trial court to summon the petitioner from jail after knowing of his detention amounts to a legal error once it was reported to the learned trial court by the process server that the petitionerdefendant was detained in district jail t t singh in connection with criminal cases the court ought to have issued directions to the jail superintendent to produce the petitioner before it this would have ensured that the petitioner was properly apprised of the civil suit and given a fair chance to respond by not doing so the learned trial court failed to discharge its duty to ensure procedural justice and thereby erred in law as an inescapable corollary of above discussion this revision petition is accepted consequently impugned order dated is setaside and petitioners application
