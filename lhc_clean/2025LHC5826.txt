discretionary order and reversed it solely on the premise that the written statement was not filed within the thirtyday period as prescribed under section a of the code of civil procedure while it is true that section a mandates filing of written statement within thirty days of first appearance the provision must be interpreted in the context of actual court proceedings not in a vacuum in the present case although the petitioner appeared on the matter remained focused on the injunction proceedings and the issue of filing of written statement was not pressed until the court on that day did not impose any penalty for delay but instead adjourned the case to specifically for submission of written statement it is a wellsettled principle of law that when a court grants time for a procedural step either expressly or by implication and the party complies within the given time no penal consequence can follow the law is equally clear that procedural provisions are intended to advance the cause of justice and not to thwart it in the absence of mala fide contumacious conduct or abuse of process a party should not be deprived of the right to defend a lis the courts must avoid adopting a rigid or overly technical interpretation of procedural rules when such interpretation may defeat substantive justice the object of section a cpc is to curb frivolous delays and abuse of the process of court but not to punish litigants who have complied with court directions and participated in proceedings in good faith wp no of it is settled principle of law that rules framed in the code of civil procedurerules are made for the advancement of justice and they should not as far as possible be allowed to defeat the ends of justice in a case titled sdoam hasht nagri sub division pesco peshawar and others v khawazan zad pld supreme court the apex court observed that the courts thus always lean in favour of adjudicating the matters on merits rather than stifling the proceedings on procedural formalities the rules of procedure are meant to facilitate the court proceedings for enforcing the rights of litigants not to trap them in procedural technicalities for frustrating their rights they are the tools to advance the cause of justice and cannot be used to cause the miscarriage of justice the honble supreme court of pakistan in a case titled manager jammu kashmir state property in pakistan v khuda yar pld sc held that mere technicalities unless offering insurmountable hurdles not to be allowed to defeat the ends of justice same view has been laid down by the honble supreme court of pakistan in a case titled imtiaz ahmad v ghulam ali pld sc reliance is further placed on the case titled muhammad sarwar v abdul ghani clc in view of the above the learned revisional court failed to appreciate the discretionary nature of the trial courts order and substituted its own judgment without any finding that the trial court had acted arbitrarily illegally or without jurisdiction such wp no of substitution of discretion without pointing out any illegality or perversity is impermissible in revisional jurisdiction furthermore the impugned order striking off the defence has farreaching implications on the valuable rights of the parties and should not have been passed in the absence of compelling and conscious disregard of procedural obligations by the petitioner as an inevitable corollary of above discussion this petition is allowed consequently impugned order passed by the learned revisional court is setaside and defence of the petitioner is
