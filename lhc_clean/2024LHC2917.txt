its assets liabilities business operations and the suits or proceedings pending against it section of the act stipulates that where a company is being wound up voluntarily or subject to the supervision of the court a petition for its winding up by the court may be presented by any person authorized to do so under section and subject to the provisions of that section sub section thereof
