resolution of scope and ambit of a suit for malicious prosecution therefore it is important to note that in order to succeed in an action for malicious prosecution the plaintiff must in the first damages to succeed in a case for malicious prosecution all these ingredients must coexist reliance in this regard is placed on raja braja sunder deb versus bamdeb das air pc abdul rasheed versus state rights and protection of an innocent party who is being victimized by misuse of the process of law and possibly that is why in the wisdom of raja braja sunder debs case supra the fundamental
