the fact that in terms of section of the specific relief act the act the jurisdiction of the courts to issue a decree of specific performance is discretionaryequitable in nature thus the court is not the supreme court in case reported as hamood mehmood v mst shabana ishaque and others scmr observed that it is mandatory for a person seeking performance of the contract under the act to seek
