KHALID ISHAQ, J.  
This Customs Reference Application filed 
under Section 196 of the Customs Act, 1969 (the “Customs Act”) seeks 
opinion of this Court on the following questions of law: 
i. 
Whether the learned Member Judicial of the Customs Appellate 
Tribunal has not wrongly interpreted the provision of Section 
157, 181, SRO 499(I)/2009 dated 13.06.2009 and failed to 
consider the SRO 1619(I)/2024 dated 03.10.2024 while 
releasing the vehicle @ 40% redemption fine against the 
law/fact that vehicle was used exclusively and wholly for 
transportation of offending/smuggled goods and found carrying 
smuggled goods falling under clause (2) of Section 2 of the 
Customs Act, 1969?  
ii. 
Whether the learned Single Bench has acted without 
jurisdiction in passing the Impugned Order, in contravention of 
the mandatory provisions of sub-section (3) of Section 194A of 
the Customs Act, 1969, which expressly stipulates that every 
appeal involving determination of duty, tax, penalty, or fine 
exceeding five million rupees shall be heard by a Special Bench
